# Governance

## Permitted purpose

The pipeline may support evidence-based allocation of finite products and staff attention. It must not become a blacklist, a press-access gate, a personal-risk dossier, or a mechanism for punishing critical coverage.

## Data constraints

- Use published professional work and explicitly authorized operational records.
- Do not collect private correspondence, personal contact details, political beliefs, protected traits, family information, health information, or off-record discussion.
- Do not infer personal characteristics from professional output.
- Do not commit a real engagement ledger or private source archive to a public repository.
- Preserve source URL, retrieval time, content hash, parser version, and correction history.
- Preserve the source and method for make, model, model-number, optional variant/SKU, vertical, category, and spec-value resolution.
- Respect site terms, robots directives, rate limits, copyright, and removal requests.

## Model constraints

- Influence must be independent of favorability toward the focal brand.
- Unverified extractions cannot enter scoring.
- Unverified product identities and competitive comparisons cannot enter scoring or reporting.
- A detected model-number-shaped string is not a resolved product identity.
- Competitive outcomes must be tied to a source location; brand-level paraphrases are insufficient.
- Negative coverage remains legitimate editorial activity, not misconduct.
- Product identity, product attributes, positioning, timing, and review method are alternative explanations that must remain visible.
- Vertical spec definitions must be versioned; changing the definition must not silently rewrite an earlier comparison.
- Small samples and intervals crossing the decision boundary are reported as unresolved.
- No model output automatically authorizes or denies a placement.

## Human decision record

Every real allocation should preserve:

- model version and evidence cutoff;
- ranked output shown to the decision maker;
- final decision;
- reason for overriding or following the model;
- product-fit and logistics considerations;
- later coverage and operational outcome.

Without that record, the organization cannot determine whether the model improved decisions or merely decorated them.
